Final Math

The syllabus gave you weights. The calculator on your phone does not know them.

Boundary: arithmetic only Final Math works from the scores, weights and targets you type. It does not say what a grade means, whether a target is realistic, or how to study. Your course syllabus decides the real rules.

The weighted truth

Scores of 90, 78 and 85 on items weighted 20%, 30% and 20% count for 58.4 points of 100, an average of 83.4% on what is graded.

The needed truth

Add a final worth 30% and a target of 80%: the final needs 72.0%. If the answer is above 100%, it says so and stops there.

The drop truth

Your syllabus drops the lowest scores? 5 scores, lowest 1 dropped: average 90.3% instead of 86.2%. You enter the rule.

Run your numbers
Final Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter, nothing more.
